# Instructor step-by-step guide

Use this guide to prepare a course before students arrive, operate the main weekly workflows, and close the course in a controlled way at the end of the term.

## A. Prepare the course before teaching starts

### Goal
Make the course ready before students begin to use the system.

### Steps
1. Open your course list at `/(instructor)/home`.
2. If the course does not exist yet, create it through the permissions available to you.
3. Open the course and go to the `sections` tab.
4. Create every section that matches the real teaching groups.
5. Go to the `people` tab to add TAs and co-instructors.
6. Set each TA permission based on the real work they need to do. Start with minimum required access.
7. Add students to the correct sections.
8. Go to the `settings` tab and configure default course rules such as lateness and score visibility.

### Expected outcome
- The course is usable
- Sections, TAs, and students are complete
- TA permissions match the actual workload

---

## B. Configure assignments and grading structure

### Goal
Give students a clear assignment list and prepare the system to store scores correctly.

### Steps
1. Open the `assignments` tab.
2. Create a new assignment.
3. Fill in the main information:
- Assignment name
- Description
- Deadline
- Assignment type such as individual or group
4. Add sub-items and maximum points for each part if needed.
5. Configure optional conditions:
- Whether it links to an attendance session
- Whether students can see scores immediately
6. Save the assignment and test it from the learner view.
7. Arrange the assignment order to match your teaching plan.

### Checklist
- Total points do not exceed the course grading structure
- The name and description are clear to students
- Deadline and timezone are correct

---

## C. Manage attendance per class meeting

### Goal
Open attendance sessions that are easy to verify and reduce incorrect check-ins.

### Steps to create a session
1. Open the `attendance` tab.
2. Create a new session.
3. Enter the session name, section, and schedule.
4. Set the policy:
- How late attendance is calculated
- Whether a PIN is required
- Whether GPS verification is required
5. Save the session.

### Steps to open the session live
1. Recheck the session details.
2. Select `activate` to open check-in.
3. Share the QR code or link with students.
4. Monitor attendance in real time.
5. Select `close` when the time window ends.

### Steps after the session closes
1. Open the session records.
2. Find students whose status is incorrect.
3. Update attendance per student or in bulk.
4. Add a note explaining the reason for the change.

---

## D. Grade during the term

### Goal
Record scores accurately and keep them auditable.

### Steps
1. Open the `scores` tab.
2. Choose the assignment to grade.
3. Select the grading mode:
- Individual
- Group
- Bulk
4. Save the scores.
5. Review the result in `score summary` or `score matrix`.
6. Check whether any students remain ungraded.

### Best practice
- Grade in small batches and review each batch
- Use a short rubric note among the TA team to reduce inconsistency

---

## E. Handle score edit requests

### Goal
Approve or reject requests transparently and with a clear audit trail.

### Steps
1. Open the `approval` tab.
2. Review the pending requests.
3. Check the main details:
- Previous score and requested score
- Reason
- Attached evidence
- Requester
4. Decide whether to:
- Approve
- Reject with a reason
5. Use batch approve or reject when appropriate.
6. Confirm the final result in score summary.

---

## F. Manage exam scores and bonus points

### Exam scores
1. Open the `exam-scores` tab.
2. Configure the exam score components if they are not set yet.
3. Record scores per student or in bulk.
4. Review high, low, and average statistics.

### Bonus points
1. Open the bonus section of the course.
2. Select the student.
3. Enter the bonus score and reason.
4. Save and verify the summary.

---

## G. Operate the review queue

### Goal
Keep in-person review or help sessions orderly and fair.

### Steps
1. Open the `queue` tab.
2. Create or open a queue session.
3. Configure queue conditions such as a PIN if needed.
4. Have TAs open the worker view.
5. Monitor the queue during the session.
6. Close the session after class.

### Metrics to review
- Average waiting time
- Number of completed queue items
- Number of canceled queue items

---

## H. Close the course in a controlled way

### Steps
1. Verify that every assignment has complete scores.
2. Check that no score edit requests remain pending.
3. Confirm exam scores and bonus points are correct.
4. Review important activity log events.
5. Close the course if that is required by policy.

### Records worth keeping
- A snapshot of the score summary
- A summary of important events
- Policy notes for the next term
