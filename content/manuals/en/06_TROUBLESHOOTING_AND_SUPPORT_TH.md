# Troubleshooting and support guidance

This guide collects common failure patterns, the first recovery steps to try, and the minimum context users should send before asking the course team or platform team for help.

## 1) Sign-in problems

### Symptoms
- Login fails
- Reset password email does not arrive
- 2FA verification fails

### Step-by-step resolution
1. Verify the account name or email.
2. Confirm you are using the latest password.
3. Use the forgot-password flow.
4. Check the spam folder in your email.
5. Verify the device time is correct, which is important for TOTP.
6. If the issue remains, contact the system administrator.

Information to send to support:
- Login email
- Time of the problem
- Error message
- Screenshot

---

## 2) Permission problems

### Symptoms
- Some menus are missing
- Selecting a button shows forbidden or unauthorized

### Resolution steps
1. Check that you signed in with the correct account.
2. Check your role in the system.
3. Check that you were added as a member of the course.
4. If you are a TA, ask the instructor to review your course permissions.
5. Sign out and sign in again.

---

## 3) Attendance problems

### Symptoms
- The attendance page opens but cannot submit
- Location verification fails
- The system says the session is already closed

### Resolution steps
1. Check that the attendance session is still active.
2. Check browser location permission.
3. Temporarily disable VPN or proxy if it affects location.
4. Check that you are within the area defined by the course policy.
5. Try another browser.

Evidence to capture:
- Course and section name
- Session name or session ID
- Time of the issue and screenshot

---

## 4) Queue problems

### Symptoms
- Queue booking fails
- Queue status does not update
- The worker view cannot see the queue

### Resolution steps
1. Check that the queue session is still open.
2. Check the PIN if the course requires one.
3. Refresh the page and enter again.
4. Check the internet connection.
5. Ask the TA or instructor to check the worker-side status.

---

## 5) Score problems

### Symptoms
- Scores do not appear
- Scores are incorrect
- Summary and detail views do not match

### Resolution steps
1. Check whether the instructor already published the scores.
2. Confirm that the score save action succeeded.
3. Check whether a score edit request is still waiting for approval.
4. Confirm that you are checking the correct category such as assignment, exam, or bonus.
5. Ask the instructor to review the score matrix and activity log.

---

## 6) Suggested response targets for course teams

1. Student cannot attend class or submit attendance during class
- Reply within 5 to 15 minutes

2. Score or course permission issue
- Reply within 1 business day

3. Account or 2FA issue
- Reply within 1 business day

## 7) Issue report template

Subject: [Course] [Issue type] [Student ID or account]

Message body:
1. Name and ID of the reporter
2. Course and section
3. Time of the issue
4. Steps performed before the issue happened
5. Error message
6. Attached screenshot
