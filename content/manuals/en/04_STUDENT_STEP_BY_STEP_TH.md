# Student step-by-step guide

Use this guide for first-time access, attendance, queue booking, score review, and the fastest way to collect evidence when something goes wrong.

## A. First-time setup

1. Sign in at `/login`.
2. Verify your account name and registered courses.
3. Open `/permissions` and allow the permissions you need:
- Location for classes or sessions that require location rules
- Notifications for alerts
- Camera if you need to scan QR codes or use camera-based flows
4. If the system asks you to change your password, do it immediately.
5. If 2FA is available, enable it.

---

## B. Check in to class attendance

### Goal
Complete check-in within the time window defined by the instructor.

### Steps
1. Open the attendance link or scan the QR code.
2. Go to `/check-in/[sessionId]`.
3. Verify the course name and session name before confirming.
4. If the course uses a PIN, enter the PIN correctly.
5. If the system asks for location, allow location access.
6. Confirm the check-in.
7. Verify that the final status is successful.

### If check-in fails
1. Capture a screenshot of the error.
2. Record the time when the issue occurred.
3. Report the issue to the instructor or TA with:
- Student ID
- Course and section
- Attendance session name
- Screenshot

---

## C. Join a review or consultation queue

1. Open `/queue/book`.
2. Select the course and active session.
3. Fill in the required information such as the issue you need help with.
4. If the course uses a PIN, enter the PIN from the instructor.
5. Confirm the booking.
6. Track your queue status in real time.
7. Prepare your files or materials before your turn.

What you should do:
- Cancel the queue before your turn if you are not ready
- If you book again later, the system will use the new time

---

## D. Review your scores

1. Sign in at `/student/login`.
2. Open `/student/courses` and select the course.
3. Review scores by assignment category.
4. Check the total score and the information the instructor has chosen to publish.
5. If you see a problem, gather evidence before contacting the instructor or TA.

Useful evidence:
- Assignment name
- Score shown in the system
- Submission proof
- Screenshot and timestamp

---

## E. Manage your account and security

1. Go to `/profile`.
2. Keep your contact information up to date.
3. Change your password regularly.
4. Review active sessions and sign out of devices you no longer use.
5. Never share your account or OTP code with another person.

---

## F. Fast problem-solving paths

### Problem: I cannot see my course
1. Check that you signed in with the correct account.
2. Check the section you registered for.
3. Contact the instructor with your student ID and course name.

### Problem: I cannot submit attendance
1. Check whether the session is still open.
2. Check that location access is allowed in the browser.
3. Try another browser.
4. Send the evidence to the instructor or TA.

### Problem: My score is not updated
1. Check whether the instructor has published the score yet.
2. Refresh the page and sign in again.
3. If the score still does not update, contact the instructor with evidence.
