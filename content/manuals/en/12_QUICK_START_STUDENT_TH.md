# One-page quick start for students

Use this checklist when you need the shortest path to attendance, queue access, and score review without missing the required permissions or evidence steps.

## Goal
Complete the main course workflows: attendance, queue booking, and score review.

## 10 main steps
1. Sign in at `/login` with the correct account.
2. Verify that all registered courses appear.
3. Open `/permissions` and allow location, notifications, and camera when needed.
4. When attendance opens, go to `/check-in/[sessionId]`.
5. Verify the course and session name before confirming attendance.
6. Enter the PIN if the course requires one.
7. Confirm that the attendance result is successful.
8. When you need to join a queue, open `/queue/book`.
9. Track the queue and prepare your files before your turn.
10. Review your own scores regularly at `/myscore`.

## Checklist before class starts
- Internet connection is stable
- Location access is enabled if the class uses it
- You know where the attendance link or QR code will be shared

## If a problem happens, do this immediately
1. Capture a screenshot.
2. Record the time of the issue.
3. Report the problem to the instructor or TA with:
- Student ID
- Course and section
- Observed symptom
- Screenshot

## Important reminders
- Do not share your account with anyone else
- Do not wait until the last minute to submit attendance
- If you cannot stay in the queue, cancel before your turn
